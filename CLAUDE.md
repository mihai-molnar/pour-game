## Project Overview

A side-view incremental pouring game in Godot 4.5 (Forward Plus), hosted at https://github.com/mihai-molnar/pour-game (branch `master`; the local folder is `pour-prototype/`). Pourer on the right, glass on the left: hold DOWN to tilt and pour, release and the pourer swings back upright while liquid already in the air (plus a little from the lip) keeps landing. The goal is to fill the glass to a fill line drawn on it — close to the line pays coins, exact pays the most, overflowing pays nothing.

**Current state: beer is implemented, awaiting playtest** (2026-10-18) — spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-18-beer-design.md`, plan in `docs/superpowers/plans/`. A third drink, sold for 150 in the shop once round 3 is over (blacked out on the DRINKS page before that). The skill is pour speed: beer makes a head (foam) — a fast pour builds a tall one that runs over (a SPILL), a trickle makes little but costs time — and the player controls speed by feathering DOWN, since the beer bottle eases back slowly on release. The glass is held tipped toward the bottle while pouring (about 35°, straightening to 15° as it fills; it tips about its mouth, the base lifting off the counter) and stands back up when the pour stops; a serve waits for it, so a beer is always scored upright, by its beer (not the head) against the line. Wet foam drains back into the glass after the pour, so the beer creeps up about 2 rows in the 2 s after the glass stands up. Every beer glass comes with its own brown longneck holding 1.05 × the glass's target, capped with a gold crown cap that pops off. Pays 20 / 12 / 6 (spill 12). Water and wine are untouched (`--sweep` and `--sweep=wine` byte-identical).

To make room, the camera zooms out to 1.65 and moves down so a strip of the bar's front shows below the counter lip. **Scoring steps** (playtested and merged 2026-10-15; asked for in the slice 2 playtest): a glass is scored by how many pixel rows its settled surface sits from the line, as drawn — PERFECT 0–1 rows, GOOD 2–3, GOOD ENOUGH 4–6 (holds the streak), MISS beyond, SPILL if it overflowed. Each step pays a fixed amount per drink (water 10 / 6 / 3, wine 15 / 9 / 5), multiplied by the streak multiplier; a spill costs `spill_coins` (water 6, wine 9), unmultiplied, and the coin total never goes below 0. The streak counts GOOD-or-better glasses in a row (resets each round): x1 for the first glass, x1.2 after the second, then +0.1 per glass up to x3 (`streak_start` 2, `streak_step` 0.1, `streak_max` 3.0 in `GameState.stats`, for shop upgrades). The multiplier sits right after the coin counter; a grown value pops up big in the upper middle of the screen and flies into place, a reset does a "denied" shake and turns into x1.

**Smooth art style** (implemented and merged 2026-10-18 at the user's request; spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-18-smooth-art-style-design.md`, browser mockup beside it, plan in `docs/superpowers/plans/`): the pixel-art look ("looks like it was made in MS Paint") is replaced by a smooth, stylized one. Rendering is `canvas_items` at window resolution with 2D MSAA and a static camera 1.75× closer (1.65 since the key art); an evening bar (backlit shelves of bottles, mirror, pendant lamps, neon cocktail sign, polished counter) behind; glassware with thick gradient walls, streaks, rim light and contact shadows; see-through liquid that refracts and tints the bar behind it; a Fredoka HUD in soft pills. ## Commands

```bash
GODOT="/Users/mihai/Godot games/Godot.app/Contents/MacOS/Godot"   # Godot 4.5.1; quote it — the path has a space

"$GODOT" --path .                                   # play
"$GODOT" --headless --path . --import               # import assets / rebuild the class_name cache
"$GODOT" --headless --path . -- --sweep             # tuning table (or --sweep=wine / --sweep=beer): hold length -> fill %, overshoot after release, settle time, spill; beer adds a feathered-pour table (aim flow -> rows at the line, head in fingers, spill, bottle left)
"$GODOT" --headless --path . --quit-after 900 --script res://tools/run_check.gd -- --check=all   # all checks (or --check=<name>)
"$GODOT" --path . --windowed --resolution 1280x720 -- --autoplay --seed=11 --shots=/abs/prefix --shot-every=1.5 --quit=70   # autoplayed rounds + screenshots at the window's resolution (the autopilot visits the shop after each round and buys ice, lime, wine as it can afford them)
"$GODOT" --path . -- --shop --coins=120             # straight into the shop with coins to spend (round 1 follows it)
"$GODOT" --path . -- --shop --coins=200 --round=3   # the shop after round 3: beer is on sale
tools/make_background.sh                            # regenerate art/background.png from tools/background/bar.html (needs Google Chrome), then --import
```

- Checks: `tools/checks/check_{drinks,scoring,glass,pourer,ui,round,shop,bubbles,arrival,view,vessel_art,stream,keys,foam}.gd`, each `extends CheckBase` (`tools/checks/check_base.gd`) and implementing `run()` with `expect`/`eq`/`near`. Success = one `check_<name>: OK` line per check (bubbles also prints `outside the glass: 0`). A crashed runner still exits 0 under `--quit-after`, so judge by the OK lines, not the exit code. Always keep `--quit-after N` (macOS has no `timeout`; a script error can leave headless Godot running forever).
- Why the runner exists: a `--script` file is compiled before autoloads exist, so it can't reference `GameState` or any class that uses it (verified on 4.5.1). `tools/run_check.gd` therefore loads each check at runtime after autoloads, calls `GameState.reset_session(1)` before each check, and fails a check on any logged script error (Godot 4.5 `Logger`), not only on failed expectations.
- Dev flags (parsed in `main.gd`): `--sweep[=drink_id]` (loads `res://drinks/<id>.tres`, default water), `--autoplay` (the `Autopilot` plays rounds and shops), `--seed=N` (seeds the drink picks so a run repeats), `--coins=N` (start with N coins), `--shop` (open the shop at startup; round 1 comes after it), `--round=N` (start as if N rounds had been played: with `--shop` it is the shop after round N, which is what unlocks beer at 3), `--shots=PREFIX` (PNGs of the game area at the window's physical resolution every `--shot-every=S` seconds), `--quit=S`. The project opens maximized (`window/size/mode=2`), which ignores `--resolution`: to test another window size, put a temporary `override.cfg` in the project root (`[display]` with `window/size/mode=0` and `window/size/window_width_override` / `_height_override`) and delete it afterwards. A 4:3 window letterboxes (a 1024×768 window shoots 1024×576).
- Motion frame by frame: `--shots` manages only ~7 fps (each PNG save stalls a frame); for a smooth sequence use Godot's movie writer, `"$GODOT" --path . --write-movie /abs/dir/f.png --fixed-fps 30 --quit-after <frames> -- <flags>` (with a temporary `override.cfg` for a 1280×720 window), which writes every frame at fixed time steps.
- Headless runs use a dummy renderer: shaders are not compiled there, so shader changes are verified with a windowed `--shots` run (grep its output for `SHADER ERROR`).
- "Could not find type"/"Identifier not declared" for class_names that exist means a stale `.godot` class cache (scripts added outside the editor): run `--import` first.
- Live tuning: run from the editor (F5), switch the Scene dock to **Remote**, select `Pourer`/`Glass`/`Stream` and edit the exported values; the per-vessel liquid look (lens, wobble, edge glow) is in `liquid_glass.tres` / `liquid_pourer.tres` (the `Liquid` nodes' materials), the per-drink look (`liquid_color`, `liquid_absorb`, `liquid_scatter`) in the `DrinkDef`. Gameplay tunables (round length, tolerances, multipliers) live in `GameState.stats`.

## Architecture

**Rendering.** The world is still 640×360 game pixels ("world px": every coordinate, speed, size and volume; the counter top is y=280), but it is drawn smooth at the window's resolution: `stretch/mode="canvas_items"` (aspect `keep`, fractional scaling, base 640×360 so the HUD is laid out in 640×360 units too), linear filtering, 2D MSAA 4×, no pixel snapping. A static `Camera2D` (`Main.CAMERA_CENTER` (300, 199), `CAMERA_ZOOM` 1.65 — the `Camera` node in `main.tscn` must match, `check_view` asserts it; `Main.view_rect()` is the world rect it shows, about x 106–494, y 90–308: below the counter lip (`Main.COUNTER_LIP_Y` 286.5) it shows at least `Main.KEY_ROOM` (34 HUD units) of the bar's front for the key row; the glasses stay clear of the HUD pills and the pourers of the coin pill (they may pass behind the timer, top centre over everything), and only the wine bottle's base may pass the top edge, by at most `check_view.TOP_GRACE` 2 world px) frames the glass and pourer; `Main.world_to_hud(p)` maps a world point into HUD coordinates (popups, keys). One world px is 3.3 screen px at 1280×720, so a scoring row is visible: what is drawn must sit exactly where the sim says. The vessels are drawn procedurally in `_draw` from rounded copies of the `DrinkDef` polygons (`VesselArt.rounded`); the physics keeps the sharp originals, so keep shapes in the `DrinkDef` and only round at draw time.

**Units.** Liquid volume is area in px², shared by pourer and glass. Gameplay truth is these numbers; waves, splashes, bubbles and the stream's look are visual only.

**Data and state.**
- `drink_def.gd` (`DrinkDef`) + `drinks/*.tres` — drinks are data (colors, fill-line fraction, vessel shapes, payout, price, garnishes, pour feel, look). Shop group: `price` (water 0 = owned from the start, wine 80, beer 150), `unlock_round` (on sale in a shop opened once that many rounds have been played; beer 3, 0 = always) and `garnishes: Array[GarnishDef]` (water: ice, lime; wine, beer: none). Everything beer added defaults to "off", so water and wine run their old paths: `glass_pour_tilt_deg` / `glass_line_tilt_deg` (the glass held tipped while pouring; beer 35 → 15), `ease_return_speed_deg` / `ease_return_deg` (> 0: the ease-out release; beer 18°/s within 12°), `bottle_look` (`BottleLook` WINE: grape label, foil, cork; BEER: red-banded label with an ear of barley, neck label, crown cap), `bottle_per_glass` (a new full pourer with every glass), the Foam group (`foam_share_slow` / `_fast` at `foam_rate_slow` / `_fast` px²/s landing, `foam_wet_share` / `foam_dry_share`, `foam_drain_time`, `foam_collapse_time`; `has_foam()`), `fizz` (bubbles per second from the floor).
- `garnish_def.gd` (`GarnishDef`) + `garnishes/*.tres` — what can be added to a glass: `id`, `display_name`, `description` (the shop line's text), `price` (ice 10, lime 15), `bonus` (ice 0.2, lime 0.3; they add up), `kind` (`ICE` falls in and displaces liquid, `RIM` sits on the rim), `key` (physical: UP ice, LEFT lime), and for ice `cube_size` 10.4, `cube_count` 2, `float_share` 0.9 (tuned so ice raises the water line ~5 rows). The shapes drive both drawing and physics; pourer shapes are authored upright. `pourer_rest_deg` is the angle a pourer is held at (bottle 55°); `pourer_glass_color` tints its glass (alpha = body tint, walls 3× capped at 0.85; tinted glass also shades the liquid); `refill_style` is `IN_PLACE` (pitcher) or `NEW_BOTTLE`. Look: `liquid_absorb` (per-px absorption, rgb: water lets the bar through, wine swallows it), `liquid_scatter` (colour the liquid gives off itself), `glass_corner_radius` / `pourer_corner_radius` (draw-time rounding), `pourer_label` / `pourer_foil` (rects on the bottle, zero = none).
- `game_state.gd` (autoload `GameState`) — coins, round number, what the shop sold and the menu: `WATER`, `WINE`, `BEER`, `DRINKS` (the shop's catalog, in order), `is_unlocked(d)` (`buy_drink` refuses a locked drink), `owned_drinks` (start `[WATER]`), `owned_garnishes` (ids), `menu` (the drinks the next round picks from, never empty), `new_drinks` (bought since the last round; `take_new_drink()` hands the next round the first one still on the menu and clears the list); `buy_drink`, `buy_garnish` (false when owned or too dear), `set_on_menu` (false for an unowned drink or the last one on), `owns_drink`, `owns_garnish`; `rng` (seeded drink picks), `reset_session(seed)` (resets all of it), and `stats`, the upgrade seam: `round_duration` 30, `perfect_rows` 1, `good_rows` 3, `ok_rows` 6 (scoring step limits in pixel rows), `streak_start` 2, `streak_step` 0.1, `streak_max` 3.0 (the streak multiplier), `coin_mult` 1.0, `pour_speed_mult` 1.0, `refill_time` 1.0. Gameplay reads these at point of use; never hard-code a value that has a stats key.
- `scoring.gd` (`Scoring`) — pure rules: step from rows off the line (`band(rows, spilled, stats)`), fixed coins per step from the `DrinkDef` (`perfect_coins` / `good_coins` / `ok_coins`; a spill is `-spill_coins`), the garnish bonus (`garnished_coins(base, garnishes)`: × (1 + Σ bonus), rounded, for positive steps only; `score(..., garnishes)` records their ids), the streak (`next_streak`: GOOD-or-better glasses in a row; GOOD ENOUGH holds, MISS/SPILL/new round reset) and `multiplier(streak, stats)` (x1 until `streak_start`, then 1 + `streak_step` × streak, capped at `streak_max`). Earnings use the multiplier *before* the glass and `coin_mult`; a spill's cost uses neither. `Main` floors the coin total at 0 (`r.coins` is what was actually won or lost).

**Simulation loop.** `Main` is a phase machine (INTRO, PLAYING, LAST_CALL, SHOP). The round's end calls `open_shop()`: the bar (Main's own canvas items) and the HUD hide, the `Shop` opens with the round line (`Shop.round_line(round, round_stats)`; none under `--shop` before any round), and `tick()` only advances `phase_time`; the shop's Space/NEXT ROUND calls `next_round()` (SHOP only). `main.tick(dt, hold)` is the only place round logic advances (tweens are HUD visuals only); each tick runs the fixed 60 Hz sim in a fixed order: `pourer.step()` returns the volume that left the spout -> `stream.emit(spout, exit_velocity, vol, dt)` -> `stream.step(dt, glass)` -> `glass.step(dt)`. The sweep, the checks and `Autopilot` call `tick()` in tight loops, so gameplay must stay deterministic (randomness only for visuals: bubbles, drips, droplets). `manual` mode stops `_physics_process` from ticking so checks and tools drive `tick()` themselves. Serving: Space queues a serve that fires once `!pourer.is_pouring()`, `stream.is_empty()` (in-flight liquid always counts) and `!glass.garnish_busy()`; serves under 1 % of capacity are ignored; Space is ignored while a glass or pourer arrives (`is_swapping()`) or while the pourer `is_busy()` (arriving, corked or refilling). Garnishes: UP/LEFT call `try_garnish(key)` — the current drink's garnish with that key, if owned, while PLAYING or LAST_CALL once the glass has landed (not during INTRO or in the shop); `garnish_keys()` lists the ones a press would add right now (owned, not on the glass yet, `_can_garnish()`) — the garnish keys on screen; `glass_has_drink()` is true while serving with ≥ 1 % in the glass and the round not ending — the SPACE key; `is_serve_queued()` (the key held down); `hold_down` is the DOWN hold the last tick used (false in INTRO and the shop). After a serve the glass vanishes with a puff and the next glass (`_pick_drink()`: a just-bought drink first, else a uniform pick from `GameState.menu` via `GameState.rng`) arrives in 0.3 s, bare; if its drink differs, the pourer vanishes too and the new one arrives full. Each round's first glass and pourer arrive during INTRO. LAST_CALL auto-serves after 3 s idle (DOWN not held, not pouring, pourer not busy, nothing in the air, no garnish falling); the last glass vanishes and the shop opens 0.35 s later (`SHOP_DELAY`); there is no separate landing trigger. Holding DOWN across the round end into the next INTRO does nothing (INTRO ignores input).

- `pourer.gd` (renamed from `pitcher.gd`) — node origin = spout lip = rotation pivot; `rotation = -(rest + tilt)` where rest = `pourer_rest_deg` (tilt, `max_tilt_deg` and "upright" all count from the rest angle). Holding approaches `tilt_speed_deg` until liquid reaches the lip, then the slower `pour_tilt_speed_deg` (careful hands); releasing applies a constant `return_force` plus damping (momentum carries it a little further, then it eases back; small bounce at rest) — or, with `ease_return_speed_deg` > 0 (beer) and not refilling, eases out of the forward tip and drifts back at up to that speed, slowing within `ease_return_deg` of rest and settling without a clunk, so tapping DOWN feathers the pour (beer's low `pour_rate` 2.5 makes a short release thin the flow rather than stop it). Pouring is geometric: `excess = volume − area(interior ∩ half-plane below the line through the lip at local angle tilt + slosh)`; that excess drains at `pour_rate` (`pour_speed_mult` only scales the tilt speeds `tilt_speed_deg` and `pour_tilt_speed_deg`, not `pour_rate`) with exit speed growing with √excess. Slosh angle is driven by tilt acceleration. Auto-refill: below 1 % of full volume the pourer swings upright ignoring DOWN, then over `refill_time` either fills in place (`IN_PLACE`) or vanishes (`vanished` signal), a new corked bottle arrives (0.3 s) and wiggles until the cork pops (`popped`) at `max(refill_time − 0.3, 0.1)` (`NEW_BOTTLE`); liquid already in the air still lands. `refill_progress()` (1 when not refilling; 0 while swinging back upright, then the fill or the new bottle's arrival + uncork over its time — the DOWN key's ring); `static rest_center(d, lip)` (its middle held at rest with the spout at `lip` — where the DOWN key goes). `arrive(d, at)` slides it in from x = 680 (a bottle arrives corked and pops `POP_TIME` 0.15 s after landing); `place(at)` puts it home at once; `reset()` also returns it home. The arrival lean turns only its `Back`/`Liquid`/`Front` layers. Drawing: `drawn_liquid_polygons()` (the liquid cut to the rounded interior, slivers dropped), the glass body in `Back`, walls in a gradient built from `pourer_glass_color`, the surface sheen, label and foil from the `DrinkDef`, a lip ring on a bottle / a rim line on a pitcher, the handle as a thick glassy polyline, the cork while `corked` (`cork_rect()`: a touch wider than the mouth, sticking out of the lip about 1.3× its width, foot under the lip ring; `VesselArt.draw_cork` draws a natural flecked cork with bevelled top edges, also used for the flying cork; the cork pops from its centre) — a BEER-look bottle instead has a crown cap (`cap_rect()`, 6 px wider than the mouth, over the lip ring; `VesselArt.crown_cap_polygon` / `draw_crown_cap`, the flying one an `Effects` CAP), its own label and neck label; `draw_reflection(ci)` for the counter.
- `stream.gd` — one ballistic `Parcel` per pouring tick. Crossing the rim decides inside (clamped to the opening, lands on `glass.surface_y_world_at` -> `glass.receive`) vs missed (falls to `table_y`, counted as spilled). While the glass is tipped (`glass.tilt != 0`) each parcel goes through `_step_tipped` instead, in the glass's own frame: `glass.entry()` (crossed the tipped opening between the lips, or the turning rim passed it), `keep_inside()` (runs down the walls), lands at `surface_local_at()` (the head's top or the liquid) via `receive_local()`. Upright, the old code runs untouched. Below `drip_flow` volume accumulates into discrete drops. Drawing: each run of parcels emitted on consecutive ticks (plus the landing point while landing and the spout while flow is continuous) is one smooth ribbon (`Stream.ribbon(points, widths)`), its width set at the spout from the flow and thinning as the parcel speeds up (`width · sqrt(speed0 / speed)`), with a thin bright core; drops and splash droplets are small discs.
- `glass.gd` — the interior polygon is rasterized once into per-column spans and per-row areas (world px), so `level_for(volume)` is exact and capacity = interior pixel count. The surface is a 1D wave equation per column, excited by impacts and by the glass's horizontal acceleration. Bubbles must stay within the column spans (`_inside`) and pop at the surface or at their column's top (a bowl narrowing toward the rim). An empty glass doesn't slosh from its own motion. The target is fixed per drink (`fill_fraction × capacity`), not random; `reset()` only empties the glass. `rows_from_line(vol)` = rows between the settled surface and the line (what scoring uses; it rounds both to whole rows). Drawing: `liquid_polygon()` = the calm level minus each column's wave, down to the floor, cut to the rounded interior (a calm glass's drawn top is exactly `level_for(volume)`; `check_glass` pins it). `line_y()` = `round(target_y)`, where the fill line is drawn; `band_edges(n)` = `line_y() ± (n + 0.5)` cut to the glass — the rows scoring treats as within n of the line; the bracket (`bracket_x()`, 3 px left of the outer wall across the band) spans GOOD ENOUGH (±`ok_rows`) with inner ticks at ±`good_rows`; the target marker — a little bolder than those ticks — thickens the spine across PERFECT (±`perfect_rows`) and runs on `line_y()` from the spine to the glass's left wall (`line_span()`), never across the glass. Three layers: `Back` (frost body, soft outline), `Liquid` (shader), `Front` (gradient walls, surface sheen, bubbles tinted by the drink, streaks, rim light, rim, drips, band, target marker). `draw_reflection(ci)` draws it for the counter reflection. `arrive(d, at)` sets it up empty at x = −40 and slides it in (`landed` signal); `place(at)`, `vanish()`. Garnishes: `add_garnish(g)` (false if already on), `garnishes`, `has_garnish(id)`, `garnish_busy()`; ice lands `ICE_FALL_TIME` 0.35 s after it is added (from `ICE_DROP_Y`, above the view; it kicks the waves and emits `garnish_landed`, Main adds a splash), the lime `LIME_DROP_TIME` 0.25 s (drops `LIME_DROP_PX` and bounces). Landed ice is gameplay: `surface_level(liquid)` is the calm level with the cubes standing on the floor in shallow liquid (it fills around them) and floating `float_share` deep once it is deeper — `rows_from_line`, the drawn liquid and the shader's `surface_y` all use it; `displaced(liquid)`; `max_liquid()` = what fits before it spills (`_overflow()` on receive and when ice lands). Once landed, the cubes move in `ice` (`IcePhysics`, a visual-only rigid-body sim stepped from `step()`: gravity, buoyancy with extra lift below the float line, water drag, bounce/friction/settling against the interior's walls and floor and each other, `push()` from liquid landing beside them); the scripted fall still ends at the landing pose at `ICE_FALL_TIME`, then the sim takes over (entry speed ≤ `ICE_ENTRY_SPEED`, a random spin). The cubes (`_cube_poses`) are drawn in `Back` (so the liquid refracts and tints their submerged part) with edges in `Front`; the lime (`LIME_RADIUS` 9, `LIME_ANGLE`) sits on the left rim, drawn in `Front` before the band and target marker so they stay on top. `show_marks` gates the band and marker (nothing turns it off at present). `reset()` clears garnishes. The lean turns only its layers, never the node, so landing liquid and the rim/opening helpers never see it. Beer: `foam` (a `Foam`, null for drinks without a head): `receive` lands beer through `foam.land`, `step` adds what drains (`foam.step`), `_overflow()` spills the head first (foam drips), `foam_level()` (the calm head's top), `settled_volume()` (the liquid once the head has drained), `foam_polygon()` (from the beer's wavy surface to the lumpy top, `FoamArt.bump`), `surface_y_world_at` lands on the head; fizz (`def.fizz` bubbles/s from 3–5 points on the floor, a bubble cap of 120). The tilt: `tilt` (rad, + = top toward the bottle), `step_tilt(dt, pouring)` (Main calls it each tick between the stream's emit and step: tips toward `glass_pour_tilt_deg`, straightening to `glass_line_tilt_deg` as the beer nears the line, rides out gaps under `TILT_GRACE` 0.25 s, comes back exactly upright with time constants `TILT_IN` 0.1 / `TILT_OUT` 0.12 s, snapping at `TILT_SNAP`), `is_upright()`, `pivot()` (the mouth's middle), `frame()` (local → node, the tilt about the mouth; the draw layers get `lean * frame()` — the node never rotates), `to_glass` / `from_glass`, `calm_y(x, level)` (a world-level line in local coordinates, `level` its height at the mouth's x). Tipped, `surface_level` solves the sloped line over the pixel columns (`_area_and_width`, safeguarded Newton; exactly `level_for` at tilt 0) and `max_liquid()` is the area under the level line through the rim corner highest in the world; bubbles rise toward world-up.
- `arrival.gd` (`Arrival`) — pure slide/lean curves of the time since an arrival started (0.3 s, ≤ 3 px overshoot, 6° lean, wobble ≤ 0.15 s after) and `pivot()`, the lowest leading corner a vessel tips over. Vessels step them inside `tick()`, so arrivals stay deterministic.

- `ice_physics.gd` (`IcePhysics`) — the ice cubes' visual simulation (above): `Cube` (pos, vel, angle, spin), `add`, `step(dt, interior, surface)`, `push(x, strength)`, `corners(c)`, `max_speed()`, `max_spin()`.
- `liquid.gdshader` — on each vessel's `Liquid` `DrawLayer`. Samples `hint_screen_texture` (linear) with a lens + wobble shift along the vessel's local x axis, turned into a screen-UV offset through `dFdx`/`dFdy` of the local position and `SCREEN_UV` (so it holds at any window size, zoom or tilt); tints it by Beer–Lambert absorption over the view path (`2·half_width·sqrt(1−rel²)`, a little deeper toward the bottom) and adds `tint · scatter`; a faint edge glow near the walls. Vessel scripts set `tint`/`absorb`/`scatter` from the `DrinkDef` and the geometry uniforms (`center_x`, `half_width`, `surface_y`); the per-vessel look uniforms (`lens_strength`, `wobble_px`, `edge_glow`) live in the materials in `main.tscn`. Godot copies the screen texture once, at the first item that reads it, so liquids only refract what was drawn before the glass's liquid (background, reflection, shadows, the glass's `Back`).

**Current tuning baseline** (from `--sweep`, water, full pourer, `pour_tilt_speed_deg = 11.5`): pouring starts with a 0.6 s hold; the hold nearest the fill line is 3.0 s (−1.0 %); 12.4–12.7 % of glass capacity lands after release for fills of 72–89 % (the overshoot the player learns to anticipate); a full pourer is 2.47 glasses at the 90 % line. Half-full pourer: pouring starts ~1.4 s, nearest-line hold 2.6 s. Scoring counts pixel rows: PERFECT (±1 row as drawn) is about ±2.7 % of the target on this glass, GOOD ENOUGH's ±6 rows about ±11 %.

Beer (`--sweep=beer`, the feathered table: DOWN on while the flow is under an aim, released like the autopilot; `pour_tilt_speed_deg = 10`, `max_flow = 1000`, `pour_rate = 2.5`, `tilt_response = 3`, foam shares 0.02 → 0.6 between 250 and 1200 px²/s, wet 0.45 / dry 0.05, drain 1.5 s, collapse 40 s): the glass's target is 1 712 px² (line 26 px under the rim), the bottle holds 1.05 of it. Aiming at 700 px²/s (the autopilot): DOWN let go after 3.7 s, the glass upright at 5.3 s with the beer 2 rows under the line and 2.2 fingers of head, at the line 2 s later, 1.2 % left in the bottle. A trickle (200) takes 8.7 s for 0.3 fingers; 900+ and flat out run the head over (a SPILL) and empty the bottle. Mid-pour, letting go for 0.3 s thins 675 → 252 px²/s, 0.5 s stops it. The bottle tips at most ~49° past rest. An autopilot round of beer earns 148 against wine's 136.

Wine (`--sweep=wine`, full bottle, `pour_tilt_speed_deg = 5`, `max_flow = 1000`, `stream_speed_k = 3`): pouring starts with a 0.6 s hold; nearest-line hold 2.0 s (−3.7 %); 15–17 % of glass capacity (about 29 % of the target) lands after release; fill moves 12 %/0.2 s near the line (water ~10); no spill up to the GOOD band; a full bottle is 2.67 glasses. Half-full: pouring starts ~1.0 s, line between 2.2 and 2.4 s. The wine glass was sized (before scoring moved to rows) so a ±3 % PERFECT was ±1 row; with rows, its bands are as wide in pixels as water's.

## Visual identity

- Smooth and stylized (see the spec and its browser mockup): a dim, warm evening bar behind; glass and pourers large and central, reading as glass (thick walls with a light gradient, streaks, rim light, contact shadows); see-through liquid; soft dark pills for the HUD.
- The shop is a printed drinks menu (the user's "BEVERAGES" reference): off-white `Palette.PAPER`, `MENU_INK`, Oswald (`fonts/Oswald-VariableFont_wght.ttf`) for titles, names, tabs and the OWNED stamp, Courier Prime (`fonts/CourierPrime-*.ttf`) for descriptions, prices and notes (OFL licences beside them), index tabs in `TAB_UPGRADES` / `TAB_DRINKS` / `TAB_MENU`, the stamp in `STAMP`; ink illustrations (dark outline, flat translucent fills), not the game's glassy art (it vanishes on paper).
- Background: `tools/background/bar.html` draws it on a canvas in world units (far layers blurred ~2.5 image px, the bottles ~1.6, the counter sharp; the area behind the play space darkened a little so the vessels read; below the lip the bar's front, calm dark wood with faint grain and panel seams, where the keys sit); `tools/make_background.sh` renders it to `art/background.png` (2800×1610, mipmapped) through headless Chrome. Behind the vessels keep mid-level detail so the refraction has something to bend, but nothing brighter than the vessels' highlights.
- Beer: amber-gold `BEER` / `BEER_LIGHT`, a little cloudy (higher `liquid_scatter`) but see-through; a creamy `FOAM` head with a lumpy top and visible bubbles, the beer/foam boundary always clear; fizz rising all the time; a dark amber see-through longneck (`BEER_BOTTLE`), cream label with a `BEER_LABEL` band, gold `CAP_GOLD` crown cap; a tall weizen glass with a ringed heavy base.
- Colors: named colours live in `palette.gd` (`class_name Palette`, started from ENDESGA 32, now with additions like `LABEL`); reference `Palette.*` rather than literals in game code. Gradients and alpha variants between them are fine. The bar's colours live in `bar.html` only (the game never reads them).
- Fill line and band: a bracket left of the glass spanning GOOD ENOUGH with inner ticks where GOOD ends, and the target as a slightly bolder marker (spine thickened across the PERFECT rows, a tick from the spine to the glass's left wall) — nothing crosses the glass; all dark-underlaid so they read on the bar. Popup text has a dark outline.
- Keys: chunky rounded keycaps in the HUD pills' family (dark base, navy-slate face, white glyph), no words on or near them; SPACE doesn't glow, it appears and disappears.
- Font: Fredoka (`fonts/Fredoka.ttf`, variable weight 300–700, SIL OFL — licence beside it), SemiBold 600 for the HUD, Bold 700 for popups and banners. The shop menu uses Oswald and Courier Prime instead (below).

## Gotchas

- GDScript `const` can't hold `PackedVector2Array(...)` (not a constant expression) — vessel shapes are member `var`s.
- `FontVariation.variation_opentype` needs the integer tag (`TextServerManager.get_primary_interface().name_to_tag("wght")`); a `"wght"` string key is silently ignored (the font renders at its default weight, 300 for Fredoka).
- `CanvasItem.clip_children`: 1 = CLIP_CHILDREN_ONLY (mask only), 2 = CLIP_CHILDREN_AND_DRAW (the mask is drawn too).
- `draw_colored_polygon` logs "triangulation failed" for slivers and folded outlines — drop polygons under 0.5 px² and check `Geometry2D.triangulate_polygon` before drawing generated outlines. Headless runs never show that error (the dummy renderer skips triangulation), so checks must call `Geometry2D.triangulate_polygon` themselves.
- `Geometry2D.clip_polyline_with_polygon` returns the part *outside* the polygon; use `intersect_polyline_with_polygon` for the inside.
- `.godot/` is editor cache — never edit or commit it. Commit Godot's `.uid` sidecars; `main.tscn` will gain `uid=` attributes on first editor save — commit that rewrite too.
